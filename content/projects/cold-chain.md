## Context

Temperature excursions in the pharmaceutical cold chain cause product waste and compliance risk. I built a simulated dataset to show how a simple, well-structured dashboard can show a logistics team where to act first.

## What I did

- Created a mock dataset of 100 pharmaceutical shipments across EU countries
- Simulated cold-chain failures with Excel formulas
- Designed interactive PivotTables and charts
- Flagged EU GDP compliance risks by product, month and route
- Summarised the key business insights with an estimated financial impact

## Key insights

| Category | Insight |
| --- | --- |
| High-risk routes | Italy → Germany (14.7 °C), Italy → Poland (13.5 °C), Sweden → Greece (13.2 °C) |
| Product risk | Insulin: 78% of shipments critical |
| Seasonality | Violations spike from May to July, especially in southern Europe |
| Estimated impact | A 22% waste reduction ≈ €480K potential savings per year |
