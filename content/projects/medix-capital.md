## Context

The Venture Institute fellowship is an eight-week programme on how venture funds work: fund structure and economics, deal flow, LP fundraising, fund operations and the use of AI in venture. Each fellow builds a fund of their own as the course project.

## The thesis

Medix Capital invests in **pre-seed medtech and diagnostics companies in Europe**. It focuses on **hospital-procured devices**, where scientific validity and reimbursement potential can be judged together.

The thesis draws on both sides of my background: science training to judge the evidence, and go-to-market work at Charité, where I interviewed clinicians and hospital procurement contacts about how a device actually gets bought.

## What I did

- Wrote the **fund thesis**: focus, stage, geography and why the niche is attractive
- Built the **portfolio model** for a fund of this size
- Wrote **investment memos** on Decile Hub, with peer and mentor feedback on each piece every week
