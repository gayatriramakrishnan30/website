## Project

We developed and presented a go-to-market strategy for a pharmaceutical product as part of a strategic marketing module in the life sciences.

## Key contributions

- **Market segmentation:** analysed four patient and healthcare-provider segments to pin down target audiences and opportunities
- **Competitive intelligence:** analysed 10+ pharmaceutical competitors and visualised the landscape
- **Integrated campaign:** designed a multi-channel plan covering digital tactics, engagement with healthcare professionals and patient education, each with measurable KPIs
