## The challenge

Evela Health is a Berlin-based digital clinic for women's health that starts with menopause care. The task was to define who to sell to, plan the go-to-market and write the first outreach.

## Approach

I used AI tools step by step for research: shortlisting, comparing, drafting. **I cross-checked every statistic** against company reports and peer-reviewed sources before using it.

**1. Target selection.** I shortlisted five large German employers with published 2023–2025 diversity reports and a high share of women in the workforce: Zalando, Bayer, SAP, Deutsche Telekom and Allianz. **Zalando SE** came out on top:

- 47% female workforce, against a tech-sector average of around 32%
- A five-year D&I programme (*do.BETTER*) with board-level ownership
- Headquartered in Berlin, like Evela

**2. Ideal customer profile**

| Category | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Company size | 500–10,000 employees |
| Industry | E-commerce, tech, life sciences |
| Location | Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt |
| Buyer | Chief People Officer, HR director, DEI lead |
| Pain point | Retention of women aged 45–55; about 6% absenteeism in German tech ([ScienceDirect, 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378512225004542)) |
| Buying trigger | D&I reporting deadlines, ESG benchmark reviews |
| Filters | ≥40% female workforce, active wellness programmes |

**3. Mini GTM plan.** The objective was to position Evela as a strategic wellness partner for retaining midlife women employees. The plan:

- Identify 25–30 HR and DEI leaders in Berlin through LinkedIn and D&I reports
- Send personalised outreach that cites absenteeism data and offers a pilot
- Share a "Menopause at Work" whitepaper with sector statistics
- Book 3–5 intro calls with Evela's CEO

**4. Outreach.** A 130-word cold email to the Chief People Officer that opens with *do.BETTER* and the verified figures, plus a short LinkedIn follow-up.

> I'm reaching out from Evela Health, a Berlin-based digital clinic dedicated to women's health, starting with menopause care. Your do.BETTER initiative and 47% female workforce put Zalando at the forefront, but studies show 6% absenteeism among midlife women in tech…

## What it shows

The deliverable shows a compact, consulting-style BD workflow: a sharp ICP, a measurable plan, and outreach where every number is verified.
