## Context

Enhertu (trastuzumab deruxtecan) is an antibody-drug conjugate for HER2-positive cancers. It was developed and is sold under a collaboration between AstraZeneca and Daiichi Sankyo. The case is a useful way to study how the structure of a partnership shapes a drug's lifecycle.

## What the report covers

- Pharma collaboration models and AstraZeneca's partnering strategy
- Enhertu's clinical milestones and its position in oncology
- The financial deal structure and revenue analysis
- Regulatory strategy across three international markets
- Risk factors, partnership dynamics and lessons learned

## My contribution

- Did the secondary research end to end and synthesised it into a strategic narrative from public data
- Drafted every section, including clinical highlights, competitive benchmarking and the financial summary
- Presented the findings in a structured, academically referenced format

## Why it matters to me

The case shows how scientific innovation, regulatory success and commercial strategy have to line up for a drug to win, and partnerships are often what make that happen.
