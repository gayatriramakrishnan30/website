## Problem

Waste segregation often fails because people find it confusing, inconsistent or inconvenient. The result is contamination and poor recycling rates.

## Solution (research ongoing)

A three-part approach:

1. **Clear bin labelling**
2. **Digital education** through a QR-linked microsite or app
3. **Incentive-based community challenges**

Together they make sorting intuitive, measurable and rewarding for individuals and institutions.

## My role

- Led the project, including market analysis, consumer behaviour research and stakeholder mapping
- Ran 20+ stakeholder interviews and structured market validation
- Built the pitch deck and presented it to the approval panel and 30+ Enactus members
