## Context

COLIPI uses precision fermentation to make Climate Oil™, a carbon-light, traceable and deforestation-free alternative to palm and fossil oils. EU cosmetics brands are under pressure to cut Scope 3 emissions and drop palm oil linked to deforestation, which makes them a natural first market. COLIPI is already piloting with L'Oréal through the Green Sciences Incubator, and its CO₂-fermentation partnership with ContiTech supports scale-up.

My question was how COLIPI could move faster from first pilots to commercial partnerships.

## Ideal customer profile

- **Organisation:** EU cosmetics brands or ingredient suppliers with published climate goals and a credible sustainability roadmap
- **Buyers:** formulation lead, procurement, sustainability manager, innovation manager
- **Technical fit:** in-house capability for lipid substitution and formulation testing
- **Commercial fit:** willingness to co-develop, pilot and co-market

## Target shortlist

| Segment | Sample targets | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium skincare | Beiersdorf, Weleda, Dr. Hauschka, L'Oréal, Rituals | Sustainability leaders with strong technical benchmarks for pilots |
| Ingredient suppliers | BASF Care Creations, Symrise, Givaudan, Croda, dsm-firmenich, Clariant | EU distribution and capacity for technical validation |
| Circular / industrial | Veolia, Covestro, ContiTech, Neste, Evonik | Circular value chain and scale-up credibility |
| Private label / retail | Mibelle Group, Fareva, dm, Boots | Market access and volume via "conscious beauty" ranges |

## Pilot framework

1. **Qualify:** stakeholder mapping, co-defined pilot metrics, samples, NDAs and technical sheets
2. **Lab evaluation (2–6 weeks):** partner formulation and testing with COLIPI support (stability, CO₂ per kg)
3. **Scale-up (6–12 weeks):** pre-commercial batches, compatibility and supply-risk checks, a joint go-to-market and PR plan

## 30-60-90 day plan

| Days | Actions | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 | Shortlist, intro calls, CRM setup, samples | 3 qualified leads |
| 31–60 | Lab evaluations, scale-up preparation | 1 validated pilot |
| 61–90 | Two pilot agreements signed, PR started | Early commercial traction |

## Risks and mitigations

- **Technical mismatch:** prioritise the best technical fits and support reformulation
- **Price sensitivity:** lead with premium SKUs and frame cost-in-use and sustainability ROI
- **Supply and scale:** phase-gated volumes and early forecasts, backed by industrial partners
