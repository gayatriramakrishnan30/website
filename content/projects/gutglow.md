## Problem

Many people live with recurring gut symptoms and try generic fixes that don't last.

## Solution

Personalised synbiotics (probiotics plus prebiotics), formulated from a user's microbiome, health and lifestyle data. They come with a tracking app and support from a nutritionist.

## Go-to-market

- **Digital first:** an SEO content hub, education on Instagram and TikTok, referral and loyalty programmes
- **Offline:** pop-ups, clinics and community events
- **Partnerships:** clinics, pharmacies and employer wellness programmes

## Business model

- Revenue from test kits, basic and premium subscriptions, corporate wellness and add-ons
- D2C subscriptions alongside B2B2C pilots
- Roadmap: foundation → development → validation → launch → scale

## Metrics

Three numbers tracked every week (reach, clicks, retention), reviewed on a fixed day so the team keeps iterating.
