## Problem

Germany generates around 1.5 million tonnes of fly ash a year from municipal solid waste incineration (MSWI). Disposal is costly and capacity-constrained, and toxic metals make the ash hard to recycle.

## Solution

Wet accelerated carbonation binds CO₂ into stable minerals and locks in heavy metals. The output is construction-grade aggregate.

## Go-to-market

- **Customers:** waste-to-energy operators. **End users:** construction companies
- **Revenue:** modular units, operations software, QA and advisory services, and materials supply
- **Value:** lower disposal costs and footprint for operators, plus a saleable product
- **Rollout:** pilot → first go-to-market → expansion with partners → scale → national rollout
