## Problem

Patients at home juggle separate devices and get no real-time, multi-metric view. Dosing support is limited and the load on caregivers is high.

## Solution

A wearable with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, designed for privacy first, paired with an app for visualisations, alerts and simple weekly reviews. The aim is fewer clinic visits and an easier daily routine.

## Work done

- Market sizing for remote patient monitoring and multi-parameter devices
- Scan of incumbents across wearables, RPM devices and platforms
- Phased roadmap: ideation → validation → development → regulatory → commercialisation
